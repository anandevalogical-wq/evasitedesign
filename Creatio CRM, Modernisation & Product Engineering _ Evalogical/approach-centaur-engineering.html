FAQ

Frequently asked

What is Centaur Engineering?

The practice of pairing one expert human with a configured AI agent stack, operating as a single, accountable unit of delivery. The human owns judgment, quality and accountability; the agents own speed, coverage and first drafts.

Why is it called Centaur Engineering?

After centaur chess, where a human and a computer play as one team. In open tournaments, well-run human and machine pairs beat both the strongest players and the strongest computers. We apply the same idea to software delivery.

Isn’t this just “we use AI coding tools”?

No. Tools are the smallest part. Centaur Engineering is a delivery method: agents are wired into the pipeline with defined roles and human approval gates, output is measured, and we tell you which work people did and which agents did.

Does AI-written code mean lower quality?

Unreviewed AI code does. In our model every change passes senior review and agent-generated test coverage, so consistency goes up because the boring parts stop being rushed.

What is the Centaur License?

Our internal certification. Engineers pass a 60-day practical before they deliver for clients and requalify every quarter, at three ranks: Rathi, Atirathi and Maharathi.

How are Centaur pods priced?

On outcomes. A pod’s scope, outcome and price are fixed before work starts. Augmented staff are billed monthly per engineer.

What stays human, permanently?

Requirements, architecture, risk calls, client relationships and the signature under the outcome. Our agents never work unsupervised.
