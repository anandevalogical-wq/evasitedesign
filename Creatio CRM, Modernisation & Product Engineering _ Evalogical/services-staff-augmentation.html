The basics

What is staff augmentation?

Staff augmentation means adding outside engineers to your own team for as long as you need them. They join your standups, work in your repositories and tools, and take direction from your leads. You get the skills without a hiring cycle; we handle employment, equipment and payroll.

Right for you if

  • You have a team and a backlog, but not enough senior hands
  • You need a scarce skill now, such as Creatio or ColdFusion
  • You want to direct the work yourself, in your own tools
  • You need the capacity for months, not forever

Not the right fit if

  • You want someone else to own delivery end to endBetter fit: Managed delivery teams
  • You need a stable team that keeps context for yearsBetter fit: Team extension
  • It is a fixed, well-defined projectBetter fit: Fixed-price modernisation and builds
  • You want to hire someone permanentlyBetter fit: Recruitment

FAQ

Frequently asked

What is staff augmentation?

Staff augmentation means adding outside engineers to your own team for as long as you need them. They join your standups, work in your repositories and tools, and take direction from your leads. You get the skills without a hiring cycle; we handle employment, equipment and payroll.

How fast can someone start?

Typically inside two weeks from the first conversation, including your interview. For Creatio and CFML roles, the scarce ones, that speed is usually why clients call.

What does staff augmentation cost?

One monthly rate per engineer, set by role and seniority. We quote after the first call, before anyone starts, with no surprises on the invoice.

How do you vet engineers?

We staff from our own delivery bench, so every engineer has shipped production work with us. You still interview them before you commit.

What if it isn’t working out?

Tell us. In the first two weeks we replace the engineer or you don’t pay for those weeks. After that, our monthly quality review is where problems get raised and fixed.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. IP assignment and an NDA are in place from day one, and engineers work in your repositories.

What time zone overlap do we get?

We schedule real overlap with your working hours, whether you are in the US, Europe, the Middle East or Southeast Asia, so standups, pairing and reviews happen in your day.

Staff augmentation vs managed services: what is the difference?

With staff augmentation you direct the engineers and own delivery. With managed services, the provider owns delivery against an agreed outcome. We offer both: staff augmentation here, and managed delivery teams when you would rather not manage the work.

What are the benefits of IT staff augmentation?

Speed, flexibility and control. You add senior skills in weeks instead of a months-long hiring cycle, scale up or down month by month, and keep direction of the work in your own team.

Do you offer offshore staff augmentation?

Yes. Our engineers are based at our delivery centre in Trivandrum, India, and work offshore as part of your team, with hours scheduled to overlap yours.

How is this different from outsourcing?

With outsourcing, a vendor owns the delivery. With staff augmentation, the engineers join your team and you direct the work. If you want us to own delivery, see managed delivery teams.
