FAQ

Frequently asked

Do we have to replace our policy administration system?

No. The policy system stays the system of record. Claims and service journeys, portals and agents run around it, and core replacement, if ever, becomes a later decision.

Where does AI fit responsibly in claims?

In intake, summaries, document chasing and drafting, under human approval and full logging. It removes the waiting and the re-keying, not the adjuster’s judgment.

Do you support takaful operators?

Yes. We build Shariah committee routing and governance gates into takaful journeys. Shariah rulings stay with your Shariah committee.

Can brokers and agents use it too?

Yes. Broker and agent portals share status, documents and renewals, so fewer calls reach your team.

How long does a first workflow take?

Typically 6–8 weeks for one process live, at a fixed price, before you commit to more.

What does it cost to start?

A fixed price for the first process, agreed after a short assessment. You see working software before committing to more.
