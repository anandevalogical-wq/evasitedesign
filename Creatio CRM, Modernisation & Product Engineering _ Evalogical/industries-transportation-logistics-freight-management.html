Shipping & Freight Management

Shipping and freight management software for every booking, document and status

Bookings and orders come in however customers send them, are checked once, and are tracked to delivery.

Book a working sessionSee what it does ↓

What it does

Book it once. Track it to the door.

Online booking and order entry

Customers book shipments in a portal with their own rates, addresses and service levels.

Orders by EDI, API and email

Load tenders, status updates and invoices are exchanged by EDI, alongside API and email orders.

Validation before acceptance

Missing details, wrong addresses and service conflicts are caught before the order is accepted.

Shipping documents generated

Bills of lading, labels, commercial invoices and packing lists are produced from the order.

Parcel and carrier rating

Rates from carriers such as FedEx, UPS and DHL are compared at booking.

Customer tracking and notifications

Customers see status and get notified at each milestone without calling.

FAQ

Frequently asked

What is freight management software?

Software that handles the shipment side of logistics: booking, order intake, documents, carrier selection and tracking, from order to delivery.

Which EDI transactions do you support?

Commonly 204 load tenders, 990 responses, 214 status updates and 210 invoices, mapped through your EDI provider such as SPS Commerce or Orderful, or directly.

Can our customers book online?

Yes. Each customer gets a booking portal with their own rates, addresses and documents.
