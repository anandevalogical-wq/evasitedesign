FAQ

Questions before you book?

Straight answers, in under 50 words each.

What happens in a working session?

You spend sixty minutes with the people who would deliver. Bring a workflow, a legacy system or a backlog. You leave with a plan. If it isn’t one you’d pay for, we say so and it costs nothing.

Can you modernise ColdFusion without a full rewrite?

Yes. We replace one slice at a time while the old system keeps running alongside, with rollback ready at every step. Your downside is bounded by design, and the old system keeps running.

Which platforms do you implement?

Creatio for CRM and workflow (we are an official partner), Celigo for integration, and AWS underneath. We only implement what we can support afterwards.

How does staff augmentation work?

Senior engineers join your team and your standups. The first two weeks are the test: if the fit is wrong, we replace the engineer or you don’t pay for those weeks.

Is my data safe with you?

We are certified to ISO 9001 and ISO/IEC 27001, and we sign an NDA before we see anything. SOC 2 Type II is on our roadmap. Certificate details are in the Trust Center.
